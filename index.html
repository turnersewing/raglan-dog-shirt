Print one page first and check that the square on it measures exactly 1 inch (2.54 cm). Print with scaling off ("actual size" or 100%). Tape the pages together with the corner marks matching, then cut the pieces. Cut the back, the front and two sleeves (flip the pattern for the second sleeve, so you get a left and a right). This is a first draft: make a test version in cheap stretch fabric before cutting good fabric.
