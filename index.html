Seams that must match when sewing

Notches: one line marks the front seams and two lines mark the back seams. Match the notches on the body pieces to the same notches on the sleeve.

Cutting: cut the back and front once each, on the fold. Cut the sleeve twice, mirrored (with the fabric folded right sides together, or flip the pattern for the second one). The collar, cuffs and hem trim are rectangles: cut them to the size shown and fold them in half lengthwise. The pieces show the seam line (solid) and the cutting line (dashed).

Printing: print one page first and check that the square on it measures exactly 1 inch (2.54 cm). Print with scaling off ("actual size" or 100%). Tape the pages together with the corner marks matching. This is a first draft: make a test version in cheap stretch fabric before cutting good fabric.
